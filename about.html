Our story

One jeep. A paper map. Big plans.

Travell Kichwa began with a simple belief: the best journeys are planned by people who've actually taken them. So we drove the high passes, slept in the camps and ate at the dhabas before we ever sold a single trip.

Today we design road trips, jeep safaris and holidays across India and beyond. Our promise is right there in our name — Search. Plan. Travel. You bring the dream; we handle permits, stays, drivers and every little detail in between.
